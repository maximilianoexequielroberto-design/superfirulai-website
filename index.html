Airdrop Flow

Airdrop → Register → Claim

The airdrop flow is simple and ordered: open the airdrop guide, choose the correct step, register your wallet, and claim manually after the official $FIRU launch. This keeps the process cleaner, clearer and easier to trust.

How the airdrop works

This site now follows one clear path so visitors understand the order immediately and do not confuse buying, registering and claiming.

1Airdrop announced

The campaign opens and eligible users are invited to participate through the official website and channels.

2Register wallet

Users connect a personal Solana wallet, verify ownership and submit the required details.

3Claim after launch

Approved users return later and claim $FIRU manually once claim is officially enabled.

Why this structure is cleaner

Clear orderAirdrop, register and claim each have their own step, so nothing feels mixed.
Better filteringRegistration helps reduce bots, duplicates and invalid wallets before claim opens.
Safer distributionClaim happens later and only for approved users, not as a blind token send.
  • ✓ Recommended wallets: Phantom, Solflare and Backpack
  • ✓ Avoid exchange deposit addresses
  • ✓ Anti multi-claim protection is recommended

Claim Step

Claim $FIRU

Claim is the final step of the airdrop flow. After launch is live and claim is officially enabled, approved users connect the same wallet used during registration and claim manually on the website.

1Connect the same wallet

Use the personal Solana wallet you registered earlier.

2Press Claim

Claim opens only when the campaign is officially live after launch.

3Receive $FIRU

Approved users receive tokens in the connected wallet after a successful claim.

Claim Timing

Claim will open after the official $FIRU launch. The exact date will be announced on the official channels once it is confirmed, so the page stays clear without promising a day too early.
